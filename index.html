I am a Researcher at JPMorgan Chase, serving as the Executive Director - Applied Research Director within the Global Technology Applied Research center. At this position, I lead the Quantum-Inspired Algorithms group, which develops advanced classical methods for big-data analytics, machine learning (including LLMs), and constrained optimization problems. Previously, I have worked as the quantum algorithms developer at the French quantum startup Pasqal, and at PayPal.

I received my Ph.D. in Quantum Computing from Télécom ParisTech, and have a Post-Doc from the School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh. I received my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees from the Physics Department at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur.

Research Interests

I have actively worked in quantum machine learning, verification of quantum devices, and secure communications. My expertise lies in building quantum algorithms for applications ranging from finance to fundamental science. More recently, my focus is on quantum-inspired classical algorithms:

  1. Randomized NLA techniques for big-data analytics: Fast provable clustering [1], coreset selection and general data-distillation, regression [2], [3].
  2. Classical techniques for fast discrete optimization [4].
  3. Tensor-based techniques for high-dimensional integration and LLM compression [5].
  4. Machine Learning / LLM: classical algorithmic techniques for explainability, compression, fine-tuning [6].
