I am a Researcher at the Global Technology Applied Research center of JPMorgan Chase, where I head the team of Quantum-Inspired Classical Algorithms for large-scale ML and Optimization problems. Previously, I was a quantum and classical algorithms researcher at PASQAL and PayPal Inc..

I received my Ph.D. in Quantum Computing from Télécom ParisTech, and my Post-Doc from the School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh. I received my Bachelor’s and Master’s degrees from the Physics Department at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur.

Research Interests

I have actively worked in quantum machine learning, verification of quantum devices, and secure communications. My expertise lies in building quantum algorithms for applications ranging from finance to fundamental science. More recently, my focus is on quantum-inspired classical algorithms:

  1. Randomized NLA techniques for big-data analytics: Fast provable clustering ([1]), coreset selection and general data-distillation, regression ([2], [3]).
  2. Classical techniques for discrete fast optimization ([5]).
  3. Tensor-based techniques for high-dimensional integration and LLM compression ([6]).
  4. Machine Learning / LLM: classical algorithmic techniques for explainability, compression, fine-tuning ([7]).
