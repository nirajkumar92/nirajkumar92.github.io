Niraj Kumar

I am a Researcher at the Global Technology Applied Research center of JPMorgan Chase where I head the team of Quantum-Inspired Classical Algorithms group for large scale ML and Optimization problems. Previously, I have been a quantum and classical algorithms researcher at PASQAL and PayPal Inc.

I received my Ph.D. in Quantum Computing from Télécom ParisTech, and my Post-Doc from the School of Informatics, University of Edinburgh. I received my Bachelor's and Master's degree from the Physics Department at the Indian Institute of Technology (IIT) Kanpur.

Research Interests

I have actively worked in the field of quantum machine learning, verification of quantum devices, and secure communications. My expertise has been in building quantum algorithms for multiple applications ranging from finance to more fundamental problems. More recently I have shifted focus towards primary topics in quantum-inspired classical algorithms:

  1. Randomized NLA techniques for big-data analytics: Fast provable clustering ([1]), coreset selection and general data-distillation, regression ([2], [3]).
  2. Classical techniques for faster discrete optimization ([5]).
  3. Tensor based techniques for high-dimensional integration, and LLM compression ([6]).
  4. Machine Learning / LLM: classical algorithmic techniques for explainability, compression, fine-tuning ([7]).
